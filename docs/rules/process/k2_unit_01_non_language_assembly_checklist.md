# K2 Unit 01 Non-Language Assembly Checklist

Status: Active pilot checklist  
Date: 2026-07-07

## 1. Purpose

This checklist defines the first recommended K non-language assembly pilot:

### K2 Unit 01

Use it when coordinating:

- K2 non-language content sessions;
- graded reading content sessions;
- frontend / dynamic page sessions;
- PM review of whether K non-language is becoming a real unit rather than a set of parallel tracks.

This is not a final all-level standard.

It is the first practical assembly checklist for one pilot unit.

## 2. Pilot Goal

The goal is to assemble a truthful, reviewable K2 Unit 01 non-language package from multiple already-active lines.

The pilot should prove:

- unit-level assembly is possible;
- the current route and renderer model can represent multiple active K non-language lines;
- PM can judge whether a K non-language unit is becoming coherent;
- future K1/K2/K3 units can follow a clearer assembly path.

## 3. Current Known Active Lines

At the time of writing, K2 Unit 01 already has:

- Language Unit 01 dynamic reference
- Non-Language Math dynamic route
- Non-Language Graded Reading dynamic route

These are the existing anchors.

## 4. Minimum Assembly Target

For K2 Unit 01 to count as a minimum viable assembled unit, all of the following should exist:

1. K2 Unit 01 non-language unit overview
2. K2 Unit 01 Math
3. K2 Unit 01 Graded Reading
4. At least one additional approved K2 non-language line

Preferred stronger pilot:

5. A second additional approved K2 non-language line

## 5. Confirmed Cross-Level K Non-Language Course Families

The following course families are currently confirmed as meaningful K non-language lines that can run across K1/K2/K3:

1. Math
2. Graded Reading
3. Art
4. Lego
5. Large Blocks
6. Science
7. Music
8. Cooking
9. Drama
10. Country Exploration

For K2 Unit 01 assembly, additional lines should be chosen from the actually approved K2 content pool, not inferred from K1 or PG/PK structures.

## 6. Recommended Additional Lines For First K2 Pilot

Priority order:

1. Music
2. Drama
3. Science
4. Art
5. Lego or Large Blocks
6. Cooking
7. Country Exploration

Reason:

- these lines are currently confirmed as real cross-level K families rather than inferred candidates;
- music and drama are strong early candidates for testing participation, rhythm, and expressive coherence;
- science and art are strong candidates for testing exploratory/process-based assembly beside Math and Graded Reading;
- Lego and Large Blocks are useful when the project wants to test construction-heavy presentation;
- cooking and country exploration may be excellent pilot additions when their resource readiness and theme fit are clear.

## 7. Content-Side Checklist

### Required

- K2 Unit 01 non-language overview exists in a stable source location
- Math source is stable
- Graded Reading source is stable enough for page testing
- chosen additional approved K2 course line source exists

### Preferred

- two additional approved K2 course lines exist
- naming is stable
- source folders are not sitting only in Downloads
- translation expectations are known, even if zh-CN is not finished yet

## 8. Web-Side Checklist

### Required

- one unit-level entry point can surface the available K2 Unit 01 non-language lines
- Math route works
- Graded Reading route works
- navigation does not imply fake completeness
- feedback context remains correct

### Review Questions

- Does the unit navigation honestly show which lines are active?
- Is the user experience coherent when moving between Math and Graded Reading?
- Does the route structure support adding another course line without redesign?
- Does the page language avoid implying that the whole unit is complete if only some lines are active?

## 9. PM Review Questions

PM should check:

1. Does K2 Unit 01 now feel like a unit, or still like unrelated pages?
2. Is the overview strong enough to connect the active lines?
3. Are Math and Graded Reading coexisting cleanly?
4. Does one extra experience line noticeably improve coherence?
5. Is a second experience line needed before this pilot is convincing?

## 10. Completion Thresholds

### Threshold A: Partial pilot

- overview
- Math
- Graded Reading

This is useful for internal structure testing, but not yet strong enough as a reference assembled unit.

### Threshold B: Minimum viable assembled unit

- overview
- Math
- Graded Reading
- one additional approved K2 line

This is the first serious reviewable pilot.

### Threshold C: Stronger reference pilot

- overview
- Math
- Graded Reading
- two additional approved K2 lines

This is the preferred threshold before calling K2 Unit 01 a convincing K non-language reference unit.

## 11. What Not To Do

Do not:

- wait for every K2 non-language line before assembling;
- call the pilot “complete” if only Math and Graded Reading exist;
- fork a separate navigation system for every line;
- hide missing lines behind misleading finished labels;
- treat horizontal content completion as unit completion.

## 12. Recommended Immediate Next Step

Choose and confirm:

1. which additional approved K2 Unit 01 non-language line will be added first;
2. whether a second additional approved line should be part of the first serious pilot;
3. which session is responsible for overview integrity;
4. which session is responsible for frontend assembly review.

The additional line(s) should be chosen from the confirmed K family set and then narrowed to the real approved K2 Unit 01 content pool.
