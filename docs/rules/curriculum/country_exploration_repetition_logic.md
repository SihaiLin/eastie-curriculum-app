# Country Exploration · Country Repetition Logic

## 1. Purpose

This document clarifies how repeated country elements should be handled in EASTIE Country Exploration curriculum design.

It is especially important because the Country Exploration course uses the same country theme across K1, K2, and K3 at the same time, but children do not experience every country in the same way across their three years.

The key distinction is:

- Most countries are designed as **parallel grade versions**.
- China is designed as a **sequential grade pathway**.

---

## 2. Standard Country Logic: Parallel Grade Versions

Most Country Exploration units, such as USA, Kenya, Germany, Madagascar, France, or Brazil, are **parallel grade versions**.

This means a country has three grade versions:

```text
USA
├── K1: 4 lessons
├── K2: 4 lessons
└── K3: 4 lessons
```

However, a child usually meets that country in only one grade during K1–K3.

For example, if a child studies USA in K1, they will usually experience:

```text
USA · K1 · 4 lessons
```

They will not normally go on to study:

```text
USA · K2 · 4 lessons
USA · K3 · 4 lessons
```

Therefore, the K1, K2, and K3 versions of a standard country do not need to form a continuous three-year curriculum. They are different age-appropriate versions of the same country theme.

---

## 3. Repetition Rule for Standard Countries

For standard countries, the same concrete element may appear in more than one grade version, as long as the activity design and level of thinking are adjusted for age.

For example, in the USA unit, **Coca-Cola-style pop culture images**, hamburgers, yellow taxis, or the Statue of Liberty may appear in both K1 and K2 if they are used differently.

Example:

```text
USA · K1
Coca-Cola-style images may be used as bright, familiar pop-culture visuals for colour, shape, matching, and playful recognition.

USA · K2
The same type of image may be used for sorting, comparing, noticing repeated visual design, or discussing familiar objects from daily life.

USA · K3
The course may choose not to use Coca-Cola-style images and instead use more advanced content, such as the Mayflower, Hollywood, NASA, national parks, or a USA mini guide project.
```

This kind of repetition is acceptable because the same child will usually not experience all three USA versions.

The design principle is:

> For standard countries, grade versions may share concrete elements, but each version must be developmentally appropriate for its age group.

---

## 4. No Repetition Within the Same Grade Version

Although cross-grade repetition is allowed for standard countries, repetition within the same grade version should be avoided.

Each four-lesson grade unit should provide a balanced experience.

For example, USA · K1 should not have four lessons all focused on hamburgers, or four lessons all focused on the Statue of Liberty.

A better USA · K1 structure might be:

```text
Lesson 1: Big city and USA symbols
Lesson 2: Food, sport, and pop culture
Lesson 3: Pop Art colours and repetition
Lesson 4: Build and share a mini USA city
```

The rule is:

> Within one grade version, the four lessons should not repeat the same main theme.

---

## 5. China Logic: Sequential Grade Pathway

China is an exception.

Because the first Country Exploration unit of each school year may be fixed as China, a child may experience China in K1, K2, and K3.

This means one child may study:

```text
China · K1 · 4 lessons
China · K2 · 4 lessons
China · K3 · 4 lessons
```

In total, the child may experience all **12 China lessons**.

Therefore, China should not be treated as three parallel grade versions. It should be treated as a continuous three-year pathway.

---

## 6. Repetition Rule for China

For China, K1, K2, and K3 should avoid repeating the same major themes.

Shared symbolic elements may appear lightly, especially:

- China map
- Chinese flag colours
- National Day
- red and gold festive decorations
- simple national symbols

However, these should not become the main lesson focus every year.

For example:

```text
China · K1
The flag, colours, stars, home, school, city, and National Day atmosphere may be central.

China · K2
The flag or map may appear briefly as a familiar opening, but the main focus can shift to landscapes, animals, rivers, mountains, farms, or regional daily life.

China · K3
The flag or map may appear as part of a display or presentation, but the main focus can shift to Chinese characters, inventions, architecture, cultural making, Silk Road, or modern engineering.
```

The rule is:

> China should avoid major cross-grade theme repetition because the same child may experience all three grade versions.

---

## 7. Practical Design Implications

When designing a standard country such as USA, Kenya, Germany, or Madagascar:

- Do not worry too much if K1, K2, and K3 share some iconic elements.
- Make sure the activity mode and thinking level fit the age group.
- Make sure the four lessons within the same grade do not repeat the same theme.
- Each grade version should stand alone as a complete country experience.

When designing China:

- First look at the full 12-lesson structure across K1, K2, and K3.
- Avoid using the same major theme repeatedly across the three grades.
- Allow only light symbolic repetition where necessary.
- Make sure the child’s three-year China experience feels cumulative rather than repetitive.

---

## 8. Summary Rule

Standard countries:

> Cross-grade element repetition is allowed, but the activity level must change with age.

China:

> Cross-grade theme repetition should be avoided because children may study all 12 China lessons across K1–K3.

Same-grade rule for all countries:

> The four lessons within one grade should not repeat the same main theme.

---

## 9. Chinese Summary

大多数国家探索单元属于“并行年级版本”。儿童通常只会在 K1–K3 的某一年遇到某个国家，因此同一国家的 K1、K2、K3 版本可以使用部分相同的具象元素，只要活动方式和理解深度符合该年龄段。

例如，美国单元中的可口可乐式流行视觉、汉堡、黄色出租车、自由女神像等，可以同时出现在 K1 和 K2，但 K1 应偏向看、指、玩、做，K2 应偏向分类、比较、连接和表达。

但是，同一年级内部的四节课不应重复同一个主要主题。每个年级版本都应该形成一个相对完整、平衡的四节课体验。

中国是例外。由于每学年第一个国家探索单元都可能固定为中国，儿童可能在 K1、K2、K3 都经历中国单元，因此中国应被视为连续三年的年级路径，而不是三个并行版本。中国的十二节课应尽量避免跨年级主要主题重复。中国地图、国旗颜色、国庆节等共同符号可以作为轻量背景或开场复现，但不应每年都成为主要课程焦点。
