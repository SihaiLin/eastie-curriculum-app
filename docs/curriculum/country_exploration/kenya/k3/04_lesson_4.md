# Kenya · K3 · Lesson 4
## Kenya Wildlife Field Station

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Kenya
- Grade: K3
- Lesson: 4 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children create a Kenya Wildlife Field Station or mini museum that brings together landscapes, animals, ecosystem relationships, movement routes, and simple conservation ideas. The final lesson asks children to organise and present what they know in a concrete, project-based way.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- organise Kenya materials into a field-station display with clear sections;
- present one animal, habitat, route, or protection idea using a model, card, or drawing;
- show simple understanding that people can observe, care for, and protect wildlife.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- This is our Kenya Wildlife Field Station.
- What did you observe?
- What does this animal need?
- How can people help animals?
- Please show your part.

Possible Child Response:
- animal needs water
- protect animals
- this is the route
- I made the map / I made the sign
- showing, explaining, presenting

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Field Station Role Setup

**Purpose:**  
To help children organise final project roles.

**Materials:**  
Role cards such as map helper, animal expert, sign maker, builder, presenter; previous lesson materials.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a field-station layout with sections: map, animal cards, habitat model, route map, care/protection signs.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose or receive roles. Each small group prepares one section of the field station. The teacher helps children decide what to include and where to place it.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Roles should support participation, not create hierarchy. Children can work in pairs if independent roles are too demanding.

### Activity 2: Make Observation and Protection Signs

**Purpose:**  
To introduce a simple conservation lens through child-made signs.

**Materials:**  
Card paper, markers, animal images, symbol stickers, simple sentence frames or icons.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare examples such as “Give animals space,” “Keep water clean,” “Watch quietly,” using icons.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children make signs for the field station. They choose an animal or place and create a sign showing how people can observe or care for it. The teacher helps with simple wording or symbols.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep conservation concrete. Avoid frightening messages about extinction or harm.

### Activity 3: Mini Field Station Walk

**Purpose:**  
To let children present organised knowledge from the unit.

**Materials:**  
Completed field station, pointer, role cards, visitor badges if desired.

**Preparation:**  
Arrange the display so children can stand by their section safely.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children guide classmates or another class through the field station. Each child or pair shows one part: map, animal, route, habitat, or protection sign. The teacher supports simple presentation language.

**Teacher Notes:**  
This should feel like a classroom exhibition, not a formal test. Accept pointing, short phrases, and supported explanations.
