# Kenya · K1 · Lesson 1
## Meet Kenya — Grassland and Big Animals

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Kenya
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 1 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children meet Kenya through large grassland images and iconic animals. The focus is on concrete noticing: big/small, tall/short, fast/slow, stripes/spots, and animal movement. Children are not expected to remember country facts; they should experience Kenya as a warm, open place with animals and wide natural spaces.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice several large animals commonly associated with Kenya, such as giraffe, zebra, elephant, and lion;
- respond to animal images through pointing, choosing, movement, or single words;
- participate in a simple shared Kenya discovery corner using pictures, props, and animal figures.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Look, this is Kenya.
- I see grass. I see animals.
- This animal is tall / big / fast / slow.
- Can you point to the zebra?
- Move like a giraffe. Walk slowly.

Possible Child Response:
- zebra / lion / elephant / giraffe
- big / tall / fast
- pointing, choosing, moving, placing animals

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Big Picture Walk

**Purpose:**  
To help children enter the Kenya theme through large, clear visual images.

**Materials:**  
Large printed or projected grassland images, animal picture cards, floor markers or mats.

**Preparation:**  
Place the images around the classroom at child height. Put animal cards near matching grassland pictures.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Invite children to walk slowly around the room with the teacher. Stop at each image and name what children can see: grass, sky, trees, animals. Ask children to point to one animal, touch the picture gently, or stand near their favourite image.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep the pace slow. K1 children do not need to answer in sentences. Pointing and moving toward an image are valid responses.

### Activity 2: Animal Size and Movement

**Purpose:**  
To connect animal recognition with body movement.

**Materials:**  
Animal cards or toy animals: giraffe, zebra, elephant, lion; open floor space.

**Preparation:**  
Place one animal card at a time in the centre of the movement area.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Show one animal and model a simple movement: stretch tall like a giraffe, stomp slowly like an elephant, walk with stripes like a zebra, rest quietly like a lion. Children copy the movement, then choose the next animal card.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Avoid chasing or rough “lion” games. Keep animal movement controlled, playful, and safe.

### Activity 3: Kenya Animal Corner

**Purpose:**  
To let children handle concrete materials and begin building a shared theme corner.

**Materials:**  
Small-world animals, green/brown/yellow fabric, paper grass, baskets, blocks, animal cards.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a small table or floor mat as the Kenya discovery corner.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Invite children to place animals on the grassland mat. Encourage them to choose where the giraffe, zebra, elephant, or lion should stand. Add simple paper grass and blocks as trees or rocks.

**Teacher Notes:**  
This activity can stay available as a centre during the week. Photograph children’s placements for later display.
