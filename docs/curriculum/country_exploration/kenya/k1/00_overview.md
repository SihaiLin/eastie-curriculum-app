# Kenya · K1 Overview

## 1. Country Character

Kenya is a vivid and highly visual country theme for young children. It can be introduced through open grasslands, large animals, warm colours, simple movement, drums, patterns, and outdoor exploration. For K1 children, Kenya should not be presented as a fact-heavy geography unit. It works best as an encounter with big animals, wide spaces, animal movement, natural colours, and simple shared making.

## 2. Grade-Level Lens

For K1, Kenya is approached through sensory experience, visual recognition, movement, and pretend play. Children look at large animal pictures, move like animals, touch textured materials, build simple grassland scenes, and create bold pattern work. The main lens is: “What animals can we see? How do they move? What colours and patterns can we notice?”

## 3. Classroom Setup and Key Elements

The classroom can include a Kenya discovery corner with grassland images, large animal cards, brown-green-yellow fabric, animal footprints, simple drums or shakers, pattern strips, baskets, blocks, and small-world animals. The setup should feel open and natural. Large pictures and concrete props are more useful than maps or written information.

## 4. Four-Lesson Map

### Lesson 1: Meet Kenya — Grassland and Big Animals

Children enter the Kenya theme through wide grassland images and large animals such as giraffes, zebras, elephants, and lions. They notice size, colour, movement, and simple animal features.

### Lesson 2: Animal Moves and Footprints

Children explore how animals move and what footprints they may leave. They crawl, stomp, stretch, tiptoe, and match simple footprint shapes to animal pictures.

### Lesson 3: Colours, Patterns and Textures

Children look closely at zebra stripes, giraffe spots, animal fur textures, grassland colours, and simple pattern materials. They make a bold Kenya-inspired pattern collage.

### Lesson 4: Build a Mini Kenya Grassland

Children use blocks, fabric, paper grass, animals, and their own artwork to build a shared mini grassland and show where animals can stand, move, hide, or rest.
