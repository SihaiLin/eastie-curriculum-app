# Kenya · K1 · Lesson 4
## Build a Mini Kenya Grassland

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Kenya
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 4 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children combine animals, movement, patterns, and grassland materials into a shared mini Kenya grassland. The final lesson is not a formal presentation. It is a collaborative building and sharing experience where children place animals, add grass, use their pattern artwork, and show what they made.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- contribute one or more materials to a shared Kenya grassland display;
- place animals or objects carefully in a small-world scene;
- show their work through pointing, naming, movement, or simple sharing.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Let’s build Kenya together.
- Put the grass here.
- Where is the elephant?
- Show me your animal.
- This is our Kenya grassland.

Possible Child Response:
- here / grass / animal names
- my zebra / big elephant
- placing, pointing, showing, moving animals

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Grassland Base Making

**Purpose:**  
To prepare a shared physical space for the final mini landscape.

**Materials:**  
Large paper or fabric base, green/brown/yellow paper, fabric strips, blocks, paper trees, tape.

**Preparation:**  
Lay a large base on the floor or a low table. Prepare simple land, grass, tree, and rock materials.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children help place grass, trees, rocks, and paths on the base. The teacher narrates where items go: “grass here,” “tree here,” “rock here.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
Use a low, accessible setup so children can physically participate rather than only watch the teacher decorate.

### Activity 2: Animal Placement Game

**Purpose:**  
To let children organise animals in the shared grassland.

**Materials:**  
Small-world animals, animal cards, paper footprints, grassland base.

**Preparation:**  
Place animals in a basket and footprint cards near the base.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Each child chooses one animal and places it in the grassland. The teacher asks simple questions: “Where is the giraffe?” “Can the zebra stand here?” Children can move animals along footprint paths.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Support turn-taking. Some children may need a clear role: choose, carry, place, or point.

### Activity 3: Mini Gallery Walk

**Purpose:**  
To help children revisit the unit and show their participation.

**Materials:**  
Completed grassland display, children’s pattern collages, photos from previous lessons.

**Preparation:**  
Arrange the display so children can walk around it safely.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children walk with the teacher around the mini grassland. Each child may point to one thing they made or placed. The teacher gives simple prompts: “Show me your animal.” “What did you make?”

**Teacher Notes:**  
This is not a performance. Accept pointing, smiling, touching their own work, or saying one word as valid sharing.
