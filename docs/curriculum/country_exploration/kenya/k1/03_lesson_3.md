# Kenya · K1 · Lesson 3
## Colours, Patterns and Textures

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Kenya
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 3 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore Kenya through animal patterns, grassland colours, and tactile materials. They notice stripes, spots, rough, smooth, soft, brown, yellow, green, and black-and-white contrasts, then create a simple pattern collage.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice visible patterns such as stripes and spots on animal images;
- explore colour and texture materials through touching, choosing, and arranging;
- create a simple Kenya-inspired collage with teacher support.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- I see stripes.
- I see spots.
- Touch gently. Is it soft or rough?
- Choose a colour.
- Put it here.

Possible Child Response:
- stripes / spots
- soft / rough
- black / white / yellow / brown / green
- choosing, touching, placing, gluing

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Stripes and Spots Search

**Purpose:**  
To help children notice animal patterns visually.

**Materials:**  
Zebra, giraffe, leopard, and other animal images; pattern cards with stripes and spots.

**Preparation:**  
Place animal images and simple pattern cards on a table or wall.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children look for stripes and spots. They place a stripe card near a zebra image and a spot card near a giraffe or leopard image. The teacher repeats simple language: “stripes,” “spots,” “same,” “look.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep the set small. Too many animal patterns will make the task visually noisy for K1.

### Activity 2: Texture Touch Basket

**Purpose:**  
To give children a sensory entry into the Kenya theme.

**Materials:**  
Fabric scraps, felt, textured paper, smooth card, brown/yellow/green paper, baskets.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare baskets with safe textures and colours. Avoid small loose pieces that may be choking hazards.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children touch and choose materials. The teacher names simple sensory qualities: soft, rough, smooth. Children sort materials into colour groups or place favourite textures on a mat.

**Teacher Notes:**  
This is a sensory activity, not an art product yet. Allow exploration before gluing.

### Activity 3: Kenya Pattern Collage

**Purpose:**  
To create a child-made visual piece for the Kenya display.

**Materials:**  
A4 or A3 paper, pre-cut strips, circles or spots, textured paper, glue sticks, animal outline templates if needed.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare simple collage materials in limited colours: black, white, yellow, brown, green.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose strips, spots, and textures to arrange on paper. They may make a zebra pattern, giraffe spots, or an abstract grassland pattern. The teacher supports placement and gluing.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Avoid correcting children toward realistic animal patterns. The goal is noticing and making, not accurate representation.
