# USA · K1 · Lesson 4
## Build and Share a Mini USA City

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: USA
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 4 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children bring together the K1 USA unit by building and sharing a simple USA city display using blocks, roads, taxis, landmark cards, food props, and Pop Art pieces.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- revisit familiar USA elements from previous lessons;
- build, place, arrange, or decorate parts of a shared city display;
- connect city, taxi, food, art, and landmark elements in one concrete space and share one part through pointing, choosing, gesture, or simple words.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Let’s build a USA city.
- Put the building here.
- Put the taxi on the road.
- Where is the hamburger shop?
- I see the Statue of Liberty.
- What do you like?
- Show me your city.

Possible Child Response:
- city
- taxi
- building
- shop
- hamburger
- I like this.
- My city.
- Here.

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Mini USA City Build

**Purpose:**  
Create a collaborative visible output that brings the unit together.

**Materials:**  
Large blocks, Lego, toy cars or taxi cards, road tape or road mat, Statue of Liberty picture card, city skyline cards, food shop props, children’s Pop Art pieces.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare zones on the floor or table: buildings, road, landmark, food shop, art wall.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children work in small groups to add parts to the city. One group builds tall buildings, one adds roads and taxis, one makes a food corner, and one places art pieces or landmark cards. Rotate children if time allows.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Do not over-direct the model. A child-built display is more valuable than a neat teacher-made city.

### Activity 2: Find It in the City

**Purpose:**  
Review unit elements through movement, pointing, and matching.

**Materials:**  
The completed city display, matching picture cards for taxi, building, hamburger, popcorn, Statue of Liberty, basketball, and Pop Art image.

**Preparation:**  
Place items clearly in the city. Prepare duplicate cards for matching.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Give a child a picture card and ask them to find the same item in the city. The teacher says, “Find the taxi,” or “Where is the building?” Children point, place, or match the card.

**Teacher Notes:**  
For children who need more support, offer two choices: “Taxi or building?” For confident children, invite a short phrase: “I see a taxi.”

### Activity 3: USA City Walk and Share

**Purpose:**  
Give children a gentle presentation experience at the end of the unit.

**Materials:**  
Finished city display, camera for documentation, optional name labels.

**Preparation:**  
Create a safe walking route around the display. Decide whether children will share individually, in pairs, or as groups.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children walk around the display with the teacher. Each child points to one item they made or liked. The teacher supports with short prompts: “What do you like?” “Taxi?” “Building?” “Show me.” Take photos for classroom documentation.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Presentation should be low-pressure. K1 children can share by pointing or standing near their work.
