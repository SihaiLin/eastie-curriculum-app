# USA · K1 · Lesson 3
## Pop Art Colours and Repetition

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: USA
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 3 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore bright colours and repeated images inspired by American Pop Art, then create simple repeated-image artwork using familiar USA elements.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice repeated images, bright colours, and simple visual patterns;
- use stamping, colouring, sticking, or arranging to create repeated images;
- connect familiar USA-related objects with an art activity and participate in a shared visual display.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Look at the colours.
- Same picture, different colours.
- Red, blue, yellow, green.
- Again, again, again.
- Let’s make a picture.
- Put it here.
- It is bright.

Possible Child Response:
- red
- blue
- yellow
- same
- again
- bright
- taxi
- hamburger

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Same Picture, Different Colours

**Purpose:**  
Introduce repetition and colour change in a concrete K1-friendly way.

**Materials:**  
Large teacher-made samples showing the same simple image repeated in different colours, such as a taxi, hamburger, star, building, or drink bottle shape.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare 2–3 simple repeated-image samples. Avoid too many complex artworks.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Show children the repeated images. Point and say, “Same picture,” then point to the colours and say, “different colours.” Invite children to find same/different, point to colours, and choose their favourite version.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Do not explain art history. “Pop Art” can be a teacher-facing concept; for children, focus on same/different, colour, and repetition.

### Activity 2: USA Image Stamping

**Purpose:**  
Let children make repeated visual patterns through stamping or sticking.

**Materials:**  
Foam stamps, sponge stamps, stickers, or pre-cut shapes of taxis, buildings, hamburgers, stars, or circles; large paper; paint or stamp pads.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a large group paper or individual sheets. Limit the image choices to two or three shapes.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children stamp or stick the same image several times. They can change colour each time or repeat one colour. The teacher models: “Again,” “same,” “red taxi,” “blue taxi.” Children add their repeated images to a shared USA Pop Art wall.

**Teacher Notes:**  
For K1, the process matters more than clean patterns. Some children may only stamp once or twice; this is acceptable.

### Activity 3: Bright Picture Gallery Walk

**Purpose:**  
Help children notice and share their artwork in a simple gallery-style routine.

**Materials:**  
Children’s finished artworks, display wall or floor gallery, small stickers for choosing favourite images.

**Preparation:**  
Display the artworks at child level. Leave space for children to walk safely.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children walk slowly through the gallery. They point to colours, shapes, and repeated pictures. Each child can place one small sticker near an image they like or point to their own artwork. The teacher uses simple review language: “I see a taxi,” “I see red,” “same picture.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
Avoid ranking children’s artwork. The purpose is shared noticing and confidence, not artistic judgement.
