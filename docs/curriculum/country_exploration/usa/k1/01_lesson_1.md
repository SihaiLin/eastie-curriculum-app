# USA · K1 · Lesson 1
## Meet the USA — Big City and Symbols

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: USA
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 1 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children meet the USA through concrete city symbols: tall buildings, yellow taxis, red-white-blue colours, and the Statue of Liberty.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice several concrete USA-related city symbols;
- point to, match, or choose images such as tall buildings, yellow taxis, and the Statue of Liberty;
- use blocks, toy cars, picture cards, or movement to respond to the city theme and show curiosity through looking, building, choosing, or simple verbal sharing.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Look at the city.
- It is tall.
- This is a taxi.
- This is the Statue of Liberty.
- It is big.
- Let’s build a city.
- Put the taxi on the road.
- What can you see?

Possible Child Response:
- taxi
- city
- building
- tall
- big
- I see a taxi.
- My city.

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: USA Picture Walk

**Purpose:**  
Help children notice concrete USA city symbols through large visuals.

**Materials:**  
Large printed pictures of a city skyline, tall buildings, yellow taxis, the Statue of Liberty, and red-white-blue colour cards.

**Preparation:**  
Place the pictures around the classroom at child eye level. Keep the number of images small and visually clear.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Walk with the children from picture to picture. Invite them to point, touch the edge of the picture, choose a favourite image, and notice simple features such as tall, big, yellow, red, blue, or green. Use repeated language such as “Look at the city” and “This is a taxi.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
For K1, children do not need to say “Statue of Liberty” accurately. Accept pointing, gesture, single words, or repeating after the teacher.

### Activity 2: Tall Building Builders

**Purpose:**  
Let children experience the USA city theme through construction and size comparison.

**Materials:**  
Large wooden blocks, soft blocks, Lego or magnetic blocks, city picture cards, and a simple skyline reference image.

**Preparation:**  
Set up a safe building area. Prepare one teacher-built sample with two or three tall buildings.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Invite children to build tall buildings. Encourage them to compare: tall / small, big / little. Place a USA city picture nearby and say, “Let’s build a city.” Children can build alone or in small groups, then place a taxi card next to the buildings.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep buildings at a safe height. The goal is not accurate city modelling; the goal is to connect “USA city” with tall buildings and collaborative building.

### Activity 3: Yellow Taxi Road Game

**Purpose:**  
Connect a USA city symbol with movement, roads, and simple pretend play.

**Materials:**  
Toy cars or yellow taxi cards, masking tape road lines, road mat, hoops or cones for city stops.

**Preparation:**  
Create a simple road on the floor. Add two or three “city stops” using picture cards or cones.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children move toy taxis along the road or pretend to be taxis moving through the classroom. The teacher gives simple directions: “Go,” “Stop,” “Turn,” “Put the taxi on the road.” Children can drive the taxi to the city, tall building, or Statue of Liberty picture.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep the road area clear to avoid running or crowding. This activity can be calm floor play or a light movement game depending on class energy.
