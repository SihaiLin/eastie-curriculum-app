# USA · K1 Overview

## 1. Country Character

The USA is a modern, visual, and familiar country theme for young children. It can be introduced through big cities, tall buildings, famous landmarks, food, sports, and strong pop-culture images. Some children may already recognise USA-related elements from daily life, such as hamburgers, popcorn, basketball, yellow taxis, Coca-Cola-style product images, cartoons, or superhero visuals.

For K1 children, the USA should feel like a bright and busy place full of things they can see, move with, build, taste in pretend play, and make with colour. The strongest entry points are not facts about the country, but concrete images: a tall city, a yellow taxi, a big statue, a hamburger, a basketball, and repeated colourful pictures.

## 2. Grade-Level Lens

K1 children explore the USA through direct visual, sensory, movement-based, and play-based experiences. They do not need to remember country facts, city names, or complicated cultural information. The main lens is: **What can we see, touch, move with, pretend with, and make?**

The USA unit for K1 should focus on noticing shapes, colours, size, movement, food props, and familiar modern symbols. Children may point, choose, match, move, build, paste, draw, or use single words and short phrases. Verbal output should stay optional and light.

## 3. Classroom Setup and Key Elements

The classroom can include a simple USA corner with large images of city skylines, the Statue of Liberty, yellow taxis, tall buildings, red-white-blue colour elements, hamburgers or popcorn props, basketball images, and bright Pop Art-style repeated images. The setup should feel playful and concrete rather than informational.

For K1, large visuals, toy vehicles, blocks, food props, colour cards, simple road mats, and repeated-image templates are more useful than maps, long text, or abstract explanations.

Key elements: Statue of Liberty / tall buildings / yellow taxi / hamburger / popcorn / basketball / Pop Art / red-white-blue

## 4. Four-Lesson Map

### Lesson 1: Meet the USA — Big City and Symbols

Children meet the USA through large city images, tall buildings, yellow taxis, and the Statue of Liberty. They notice colours, shapes, size, and movement, then build or arrange a simple city scene.

### Lesson 2: USA Food and Everyday Pop Culture

Children explore familiar USA-related daily-life elements such as hamburgers, fries, popcorn, drinks, and product-style images through pretend play, sorting, matching, and simple role play.

### Lesson 3: Pop Art Colours and Repetition

Children look at bright repeated images and strong colours, then create a simple Pop Art-inspired artwork using repeated shapes, food images, taxis, or city symbols.

### Lesson 4: Build and Share a Mini USA City

Children bring together the K1 USA experience by building a small USA city display with blocks, roads, taxis, landmark cards, food corners, and children’s Pop Art pieces. They point to or share one thing they made or liked.
