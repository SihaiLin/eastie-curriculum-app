# USA · K2 · Lesson 3
## Pop Art Studio — Repeat, Change, Compare

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: USA
- Grade: K2
- Lesson: 3 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore Pop Art-inspired repetition by creating repeated images with different colours, then comparing what stays the same and what changes.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice repetition, colour change, and same/different in visual images;
- create repeated pictures using familiar USA elements;
- compare their repeated images with a partner or group and prepare work for a class display.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Same picture, different colours.
- What is the same?
- What is different?
- Repeat the picture.
- Change the colour.
- Put four pictures together.
- Which colour do you like?

Possible Child Response:
- same / different
- repeat
- red / blue / yellow / green
- taxi / hamburger / building
- I like red.
- It is different.

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Same and Different Pop Images

**Purpose:**  
Help children understand repetition and variation before making their own artwork.

**Materials:**  
Teacher-made repeated-image samples using simple shapes or USA elements: taxi, hamburger, building, star, basketball.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare two samples: one with the same image and same colour, one with the same image in different colours.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Show the samples and ask children what is the same and what is different. Children point to repeated objects, repeated colours, and changed colours. Use simple comparison language.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Avoid detailed art-history explanation. For K2, Pop Art is mainly a route into repetition, colour, and visual comparison.

### Activity 2: Four-Square Pop Art

**Purpose:**  
Let children create a simple repeated-image artwork.

**Materials:**  
Four-square paper templates, stamps or pre-cut images, markers, crayons, glue, coloured paper.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare templates with four boxes. Offer two or three image options only.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose one image and repeat it in four boxes. They change the colour, background, or pattern in each box. The teacher supports with prompts: “Same taxi, different colour,” “Repeat again.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
K2 children can make choices independently but may need help keeping the same image across all four boxes.

### Activity 3: Pop Art Pair Compare

**Purpose:**  
Encourage children to compare and share their artwork in simple ways.

**Materials:**  
Finished four-square artworks, display board, sticky notes or small stickers.

**Preparation:**  
Pair children or small groups. Prepare a display area.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children compare their artwork with a partner. They find one thing that is the same and one thing that is different. Then they place their work on the class USA art display.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Support children with sentence frames if needed: “Same colour,” “Different taxi,” “I like blue.”
