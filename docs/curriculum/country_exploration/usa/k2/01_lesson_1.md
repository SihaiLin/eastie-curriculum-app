# USA · K2 · Lesson 1
## USA Symbols — Match and Sort

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: USA
- Grade: K2
- Lesson: 1 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore USA city symbols by matching, sorting, and comparing landmarks, buildings, taxis, bridges, and flag colours.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- recognise several concrete USA-related city and landmark elements;
- sort images by simple categories such as landmark, transport, building, and colour;
- compare features such as tall/short, big/small, and same/different, using short phrases to describe or choose country elements.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- What can you see?
- This is a landmark.
- This is a taxi.
- This building is tall.
- Are they the same or different?
- Put the taxi with transport.
- Put the statue with landmarks.
- Which one is taller?

Possible Child Response:
- taxi / building / bridge / statue
- same / different
- tall / short / big
- I see a taxi.
- It is tall.
- This is a bridge.

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: USA Symbol Match

**Purpose:**  
Help children recognise and match concrete USA symbols.

**Materials:**  
Pairs of picture cards: Statue of Liberty, yellow taxi, city skyline, Golden Gate Bridge, hamburger, basketball, USA flag colours.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare two copies of each image. Place one set on a wall or board and one set in a basket.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose a card from the basket and find the matching image on the wall. After matching, the teacher asks a simple noticing question: “Is it tall?” “Is it a car?” “Is it a bridge?”

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep the match set small at first. Add more cards if the class is confident.

### Activity 2: Sort the USA Table

**Purpose:**  
Support K2 classification and category thinking.

**Materials:**  
Picture cards and four category trays: Landmarks, Transport, Buildings, Food / Sports.

**Preparation:**  
Label trays with words and icons. Place mixed cards on the table.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children sort the cards into category trays. The teacher models one or two examples: “Taxi — transport,” “Statue — landmark.” Children continue in pairs or small groups. After sorting, review each tray together.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Do not worry if children debate categories. Use the discussion as a comparison opportunity.

### Activity 3: Tall City, Long Bridge

**Purpose:**  
Connect USA city images with simple comparison and construction.

**Materials:**  
Blocks, Lego, bridge image, skyline image, road pieces or tape, toy cars.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare one building zone and one bridge/road zone.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children build tall buildings and a long bridge or road. Invite them to compare: “Which is taller?” “Which is longer?” “Where can the taxi go?” Place city and bridge cards near the models.

**Teacher Notes:**  
K2 children can begin to use comparative language, but the main value is building and noticing, not formal measurement.
