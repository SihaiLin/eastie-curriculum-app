# USA · K2 · Lesson 2
## Food, Sports, and Everyday Life

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: USA
- Grade: K2
- Lesson: 2 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore familiar USA-related food and sports by sorting, matching, comparing, and joining simple role play.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- identify familiar food and sport elements linked with USA popular culture;
- sort food and sport images by simple features;
- match sports with equipment or actions and express simple preferences through words, gestures, or short phrases.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- What food can you see?
- Do you like hamburgers?
- Put the food here.
- Put the sports here.
- This is basketball.
- What do we need for baseball?
- Same or different?
- Which one do you like?

Possible Child Response:
- hamburger / popcorn / fries
- basketball / baseball
- I like basketball.
- I like hamburgers.
- food / sport
- same / different

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Food or Sport?

**Purpose:**  
Help children sort familiar USA-related images into meaningful groups.

**Materials:**  
Food cards: hamburger, fries, hot dog, popcorn, drink. Sport cards: basketball, baseball, football, ball, bat, hoop.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare two large sorting mats: Food and Sports. Use icons to support non-readers.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children take turns choosing a card and placing it on the correct mat. After each placement, the teacher asks a simple follow-up: “Do you like it?” “Can you play it?” “Is it food?”

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep American football optional; basketball and baseball are easier for children to understand and act out.

### Activity 2: USA Snack Shop

**Purpose:**  
Use food props for social role play, choosing, and preference language.

**Materials:**  
Toy food or paper food props, trays, plates, cups, shop sign, optional pretend menu with pictures.

**Preparation:**  
Set up a small snack shop with two or three food choices.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose food from a picture menu and place it on a tray. The teacher models: “A hamburger, please,” “Here you are,” “Thank you,” “I like popcorn.” Children can take turns being helper and customer.

**Teacher Notes:**  
This is pretend play, not an eating activity. If real food is used, check school policies and allergies first.

### Activity 3: Basketball and Baseball Actions

**Purpose:**  
Connect USA sports with movement, action words, and simple equipment matching.

**Materials:**  
Soft balls, hoop or basket, bat picture, baseball glove picture, sport cards, cones.

**Preparation:**  
Set up a safe movement area. Use soft balls only.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Show sport cards and act out simple movements: bounce, throw, catch, hit, run. Children match a sport card with an action or object. Then they try a simple basketball throw or baseball-style pretend swing without hard equipment.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Prioritise safety and turn-taking. Keep the activity about movement and recognition, not sport skill training.
