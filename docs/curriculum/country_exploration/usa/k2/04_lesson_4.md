# USA · K2 · Lesson 4
## Make a USA Discovery Board

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: USA
- Grade: K2
- Lesson: 4 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children organise what they have explored about the USA into a class discovery board using simple categories: City, Landmarks, Food, Sports, and Art.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- revisit USA elements from the previous lessons;
- sort and organise images into clear categories;
- contribute to a shared class display and share one simple discovery through pointing, choosing, single words, or short phrases.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Let’s make a USA board.
- Where does this go?
- Is it food or sport?
- Put the taxi in the city.
- Put the hamburger with food.
- What do you remember?
- Tell me one thing.

Possible Child Response:
- city / food / sport / art
- taxi / hamburger / basketball
- It is food.
- I remember the taxi.
- I like Pop Art.

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Remember and Choose

**Purpose:**  
Help children revisit familiar USA elements before organising them.

**Materials:**  
A mixed basket of picture cards from Lessons 1–3.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a manageable set of 12–16 cards. Include city, food, sport, and art elements.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children sit in a circle. Each child chooses one card from the basket. The teacher asks: “What is it?” or gives a two-choice prompt. Children hold their cards for the next sorting activity.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Do not test memory harshly. The point is recognition and re-entry into the theme.

### Activity 2: Category Board Sorting

**Purpose:**  
Create the main K2 unit output through classification and group organisation.

**Materials:**  
Large display board or paper, category headers with icons: City, Landmarks, Food, Sports, Art; picture cards; glue or reusable tape.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare the board with visible sections. Place headers before class or add them with children.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children place their cards onto the board in the correct category. Discuss uncertain cards together: “Is basketball sport or food?” “Is the taxi city or food?” Review each section when finished.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Some cards can belong to more than one category. Accept reasonable explanations, especially from stronger children.

### Activity 3: USA Board Walk

**Purpose:**  
Give children a simple sharing and review experience.

**Materials:**  
Completed discovery board, pointer stick if desired, camera for documentation.

**Preparation:**  
Display the board at child eye level. Decide whether children will share individually or in small groups.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children walk to the board and point to one thing they remember or like. The teacher supports with prompts: “Show me food,” “Find a taxi,” “What do you like?” Children can say a word or short phrase.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep sharing quick and low-pressure. K2 children can begin to explain categories, but explanation should be brief.
