# USA · K2 Overview

## 1. Country Character

The USA is a modern and highly recognisable country theme. It can be introduced through large cities, famous landmarks, popular food, sports, everyday visual culture, and bright repeated images. For young children, the USA has many concrete entry points: the Statue of Liberty, tall city buildings, yellow taxis, hamburgers, popcorn, basketball, baseball, bridges, and Pop Art-style images.

The USA works well as a country theme because it allows children to connect things they may already know from daily life with a wider place in the world. It should not be treated mainly as a map or fact-memorisation unit. It is more useful as a theme of city symbols, familiar global objects, visual culture, sports, and modern life.

## 2. Grade-Level Lens

K2 children explore the USA through sorting, comparing, matching, and making simple connections. The main lens is: **What is the same, what is different, and how do these things belong together?**

K2 children can compare tall and short buildings, match landmarks with city scenes, sort food and sport images, notice repeated Pop Art patterns, and build simple connections between city life, food culture, and visual symbols. They can use short phrases, simple preference language, and basic comparison language, but the country theme should still remain concrete and activity-based.

## 3. Classroom Setup and Key Elements

The classroom can include a USA discovery wall with several clear sections: city and landmarks, food and daily life, sports and movement, and Pop Art colours. Teachers can display large visuals of the Statue of Liberty, New York-style skylines, Golden Gate Bridge, yellow taxis, hamburgers, popcorn, basketball, baseball, and repeated colourful images.

For K2, materials should invite sorting and comparison: image cards, matching sets, category baskets, building materials, road pieces, food cards, sport cards, and repeated-image templates.

Key elements: Statue of Liberty / city skyline / Golden Gate Bridge / yellow taxi / hamburger / popcorn / basketball / baseball / Pop Art

## 4. Four-Lesson Map

### Lesson 1: USA Symbols — Match and Sort

Children meet USA city symbols and sort them into simple groups: buildings, transport, landmarks, colours, and city objects. They compare tall/short, big/small, and same/different.

### Lesson 2: Food, Sports, and Everyday Life

Children explore familiar USA-related food and sports through sorting, choosing, and role play. They compare food items, match sports with equipment, and talk about what they like.

### Lesson 3: Pop Art Studio — Repeat, Change, Compare

Children explore repeated images and bright colours. They create Pop Art-inspired work while noticing same/different, repeated patterns, and colour changes.

### Lesson 4: Make a USA Discovery Board

Children organise the unit content into a class discovery board with categories such as City, Food, Sports, and Art. They choose images, sort them, place them, and share one discovery.
