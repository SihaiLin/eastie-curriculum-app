# China · K3 · Lesson 4
## Modern China: Trains, Bridges and Cities

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: China
- Grade: K3
- Lesson: 4 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore modern China through high-speed trains, bridges, metro systems, towers, and city skylines. They connect modern building and design with the broader idea that people in China continue to create useful and impressive things.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:

- recognise selected modern Chinese creations such as high-speed rail, bridges, metro, towers, or city buildings;
- understand simple functions: trains move people, bridges help people cross, cities need roads and stations;
- build or design a modern China transport or city model and present one part of the class project using simple explanation.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:

- Look at the train.
- It is fast.
- This is a bridge.
- The bridge goes over the river.
- This is a city.
- Where is the station?
- Let’s build a route.
- The train goes from here to here.
- What did your group build?
- Tell us one thing about modern China.

Possible Child Response:

- Fast train.
- This is a bridge.
- The train goes here.
- This is our city.
- We built a station.
- The bridge is over the river.
- People can go across.
- I made a tower.

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Modern China Picture Sort

**Purpose:**  
Help children distinguish different kinds of modern creations and their uses.

**Materials:**  
Picture cards of high-speed trains, metro, bridges, towers, city skylines, roads, stations, and people travelling. Sorting mats labelled transport, bridge, city, or building.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare clear, child-friendly images. Keep categories simple and visual.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children look at modern China images and sort them into categories. The teacher asks simple function questions: What moves? What helps people cross? What is tall? Where do people go?

**Teacher Notes:**  
Use Shenzhen as a familiar example where appropriate, but do not make the lesson only about Shenzhen. The focus is modern making and function.

### Activity 2: High-Speed Rail Route Build

**Purpose:**  
Support spatial planning and cause-function thinking through transport play.

**Materials:**  
Train toys or paper train cards, track pieces or paper strips, station cards, city cards, blocks, map base paper, arrows, and tape.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a large table or floor route area. Make 3–4 station cards, such as City A, City B, Mountain Station, River Station, or Shenzhen.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children build a train route between stations. They decide where the train starts, where it goes, and what it passes. The teacher prompts: Does the train need a bridge? Where is the station? How can people get on?

**Teacher Notes:**  
The goal is route planning and explanation, not accurate railway knowledge. Encourage children to show direction and sequence.

### Activity 3: Created China Mini Museum

**Purpose:**  
Bring together the K3 China unit through project display and presentation.

**Materials:**  
Children’s work from previous lessons, character cards, paper/fabric/pattern pieces, architecture models, modern transport models, display tables, labels, and photo documentation.

**Preparation:**  
Set up 3–4 museum sections: Writing, Materials and Patterns, Traditional Buildings, Modern China. Prepare simple labels and display spaces.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children place their work in the mini museum. In groups, they choose one item to present. Each group explains what it is and why people use or make it. The class walks through the museum and listens to each group.

**Teacher Notes:**  
This activity should connect ancient and modern creation without creating a formal history timeline. K3 children can present short explanations, but the focus remains on visible work, design, use, and pride in making.
