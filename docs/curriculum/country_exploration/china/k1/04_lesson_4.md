# China · K1 · Lesson 4
## My China Celebration Wall

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: China
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 4 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children bring together their K1 China experiences by creating a shared class display with colours, stars, city and school images, food drawings, photos, and child-made decorations.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:

- review familiar China elements from previous lessons;
- choose, make, or place one contribution on the class celebration wall;
- participate in a shared display-building process and show ownership by pointing to or sharing their own work.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:

- This is our China wall.
- What can you see?
- I see red.
- I see a star.
- I see our school.
- I see noodles.
- Put your picture here.
- This is your work.
- Let’s look together.
- Well done.

Possible Child Response:

- red
- star
- school
- city
- food
- noodles
- my picture
- I see a star.
- I made this.

Children may also respond by pointing to their own work, choosing a display position, clapping for classmates, or walking through the display.

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Choose and Review

**Purpose:**  
Help children revisit the main K1 China elements before making the final display.

**Materials:**  
Photos and materials from Lessons 1–3: red and gold items, stars, city/school photos, food props or food cards, children’s previous artwork.

**Preparation:**  
Place previous lesson materials in four simple areas: colours/stars, city/school, food, and children’s work.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Invite children to walk around and choose one item they remember or like. The teacher asks simple questions: “What can you see?” “Red or gold?” “School or food?” Children can point, hold, name, or place their chosen item in a basket for the final display.

**Teacher Notes:**  
This activity helps the lesson begin from memory and recognition. Do not quiz children or require correct answers.

### Activity 2: Make a China Wall Piece

**Purpose:**  
Give each child a visible personal contribution to the class display.

**Materials:**  
Small paper cards, red and gold paper, star stickers, crayons, markers, glue sticks, printed mini photos or simple templates if needed.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a simple card for each child. Offer a few options: star card, school drawing card, food drawing card, or city building card.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children create one small piece for the China wall. They may draw a school, colour a star, make a red-gold pattern, stick food images, or decorate a simple city building shape.

The teacher circulates and uses simple language: “Red,” “Star,” “Your picture,” “Put it here,” and “Beautiful work.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep the craft short and achievable. The value is child participation, not a polished teacher-made display.

### Activity 3: Class Gallery Walk

**Purpose:**  
Let children experience the completed display as a shared class outcome.

**Materials:**  
Completed China wall, pointer stick if useful, camera for documentation, optional soft background music.

**Preparation:**  
Arrange the wall so children’s work is at their eye level where possible. Leave space for children to stand or walk safely.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Gather children in front of the wall. Walk slowly along the display and point to different parts: stars, school, city, food, and children’s own pieces. Invite children to find their work or point to something they like.

The teacher can close with a simple class line: “This is our China wall.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
This should not become a formal presentation. For K1, pointing to one item or standing proudly by their work is already a meaningful outcome.
