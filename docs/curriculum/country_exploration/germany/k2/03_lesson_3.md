# Germany · K2 · Lesson 3
## Castles, Houses and Building Shapes

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K2
- Lesson: 3 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore German castles, town houses, roofs, towers, walls, bridges, doors, and windows. The focus is on observing and comparing visible building features, then using those features in construction.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice visible features of castles and houses, including towers, walls, roofs, doors, and windows;
- sort or compare buildings by shape, size, function, or repeated features;
- construct a castle or town building using selected architectural features.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- This building has a tower.
- This house has a roof and windows.
- Is it tall or short?
- What is the same? What is different?
- Build a wall. Add a gate.

Possible Child Response:
- tower / wall / roof / door / window / bridge
- tall / short / big / small
- same / different / castle / house

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Building Feature Hunt

**Purpose:**  
To guide children to observe architectural details.

**Materials:**  
Large images of German castles, timber-style town houses, bridges, doors, roofs; feature cards.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare feature cards showing tower, roof, wall, gate, door, window, bridge.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Show one building image. Children match feature cards to what they can see. They point to the tower, roof, window, or gate in the image.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Use clear images with strong shapes. Avoid lengthy historical explanations.

### Activity 2: Castle or House Sort

**Purpose:**  
To help children compare structures by function and appearance.

**Materials:**  
Building picture cards, two hoops or trays labelled castle and house, optional third tray for bridge/gate.

**Preparation:**  
Mix building cards on a table or floor mat.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children sort images into castle or house. Ask what helped them decide: tower, roof, wall, many windows, big gate.

**Teacher Notes:**  
The purpose is comparison, not perfect classification. Some buildings can be discussed together.

### Activity 3: Build with Features

**Purpose:**  
To turn observation into construction.

**Materials:**  
Wooden blocks, Lego, cardboard rectangles and triangles, tubes for towers, bridge pieces.

**Preparation:**  
Set out materials grouped by possible features: walls, towers, roofs, gates, bridges.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose a feature card and build that part first. Then groups combine parts into one castle or town street.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Ask children to name or point to the feature they used. Do not require a realistic model.
