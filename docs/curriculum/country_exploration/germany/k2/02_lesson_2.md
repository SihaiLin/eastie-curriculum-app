# Germany · K2 · Lesson 2
## Forests, Animals and Small Towns

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K2
- Lesson: 2 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore Germany through forests and small towns. The lesson focuses on connections: forests have trees and animals; towns have houses, paths, and people; some places are close to nature. Children sort, match, and build a forest-town relationship.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- identify and sort simple forest and town elements such as trees, animals, houses, paths, and people;
- connect animals and objects to suitable places in a forest-town scene;
- compare natural and built elements using simple same/different language.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Is this from the forest or the town?
- Trees are in the forest.
- Houses are in the town.
- The path connects the forest and the town.
- What is natural? What is built?

Possible Child Response:
- forest / town / tree / house / path / animal
- same / different
- in the forest / in the town / near the trees

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Forest or Town Sort

**Purpose:**  
To help children classify natural and built elements.

**Materials:**  
Picture cards or objects: trees, animals, mushrooms, houses, roofs, roads, people, shops, paths.

**Preparation:**  
Set up two large areas labelled with pictures: forest and town.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children sort cards into forest or town. For objects that can connect both areas, such as paths or people, the teacher invites children to place them between the two areas.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Do not force one correct answer for every card. Some cards can support discussion.

### Activity 2: Build a Forest-Town Edge

**Purpose:**  
To make the relationship between nature and settlement visible.

**Materials:**  
Blocks, green fabric, paper trees, house pieces, road strips, path markers, small animals.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a large base with one side for forest and one side for town.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children build trees and animals on one side, houses and roads on the other, then add paths to connect them. Ask where people might walk and where animals might stay.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep animal content simple and non-threatening. Focus on place relationships.

### Activity 3: Same and Different Picture Talk

**Purpose:**  
To support visual comparison using real or illustrated Germany images.

**Materials:**  
Images of German forests, small towns, timber-style houses, paths, and trees.

**Preparation:**  
Choose four to six clear images with obvious visual contrasts.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Show two images at a time. Children identify what is the same and what is different: trees, houses, roads, roofs, colours, animals, people.

**Teacher Notes:**  
This should remain conversational. One-word responses are enough.
