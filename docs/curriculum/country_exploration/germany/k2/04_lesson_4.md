# Germany · K2 · Lesson 4
## Clocks, Wheels and Simple Mechanisms

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K2
- Lesson: 4 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore Germany through cuckoo-clock visuals, wheels, gears, turning parts, and simple cause-and-effect movement. The focus is not formal mechanics, but noticing that some objects move because parts turn, connect, open, close, or swing.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice simple moving parts such as wheels, gears, clock hands, doors, and swinging pieces;
- test cause-and-effect actions including turn, push, open, close, and swing;
- add a simple moving or pretend-moving element to a Germany-themed class display.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Turn the wheel.
- The hand moves.
- Open the little door.
- Push here. What happens?
- It moves / it stops.

Possible Child Response:
- wheel / clock / door / turn / open / close
- move / stop / fast / slow
- turning, pushing, opening, closing

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Moving Parts Table

**Purpose:**  
To let children investigate movement through safe, simple objects.

**Materials:**  
Toy wheels, simple gears, cardboard clock hands with split pins, hinged cardboard doors, spinning tops or safe turning objects.

**Preparation:**  
Set up small stations where children can turn, open, close, push, and spin.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children visit each station and test one action. The teacher names the action and result: “turn,” “open,” “it moves,” “it stops.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
Check all small parts carefully. Use only age-safe materials.

### Activity 2: Cuckoo Clock Picture Build

**Purpose:**  
To connect German clock imagery with shape and movement.

**Materials:**  
Cuckoo-clock image, cardboard house shape, clock face, movable paper hands, small paper door, glue or tape.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a large class clock base or several group bases.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children add roof, clock face, hands, and a small door to the base. The teacher demonstrates how the hands can move or the door can open.

**Teacher Notes:**  
This is an inspired classroom craft, not a detailed study of clockmaking.

### Activity 3: Add Movement to Germany Town

**Purpose:**  
To integrate mechanism play into the previous Germany elements.

**Materials:**  
Completed or partial Germany display, toy wheels, paper gates, hinged doors, train/car pieces, split pins.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare two or three possible places to add movement: a gate, clock, wheel, or train station door.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose one moving element and add it to the display. They test it and show a friend: open the gate, turn the wheel, move the clock hands.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Support children who prefer observation. Watching and describing are valid participation.
