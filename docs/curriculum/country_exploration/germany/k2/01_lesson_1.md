# Germany · K2 · Lesson 1
## Roads, Cars and Trains — Sort and Compare

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K2
- Lesson: 1 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore Germany through transport systems: cars, trains, roads, railways, bridges, tunnels, and stations. The focus is on sorting and comparing how different transport materials move and what kinds of routes they need.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- sort transport elements into road, rail, bridge, tunnel, and station groups;
- compare cars and trains using simple features such as wheels, tracks, speed, and route;
- build a connected route and explain simple movement choices through words, gestures, or placement.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Is this for the road or the track?
- Cars go on roads. Trains go on tracks.
- This bridge goes over. This tunnel goes under.
- What is the same? What is different?
- Where should the station go?

Possible Child Response:
- road / track / bridge / tunnel / station
- same / different / over / under
- car on the road / train on the track

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Transport Sorting Trays

**Purpose:**  
To support classification of transport objects and route parts.

**Materials:**  
Toy cars, trains, track pieces, road strips, bridge pieces, tunnel pieces, sorting trays or hoops.

**Preparation:**  
Label trays with pictures: road, track, bridge, tunnel, station.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose objects one by one and place them into matching trays. After sorting, compare two groups: cars and trains, roads and tracks, bridges and tunnels.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Encourage reasoning but accept simple responses. A child may justify by action: placing a train on track demonstrates understanding.

### Activity 2: Build Two Routes

**Purpose:**  
To compare road routes and rail routes through construction.

**Materials:**  
Road tape, train tracks, cars, trains, bridges, tunnels, blocks.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a large mat with space for two routes.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children build one car road and one train track. Add a bridge or tunnel to each route. Test vehicles on both routes and discuss what works or does not work.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Use problems as learning moments: “The train cannot go here. What does it need?”

### Activity 3: Station Match and Move

**Purpose:**  
To connect route design with function.

**Materials:**  
Small station signs, people figures, vehicle toys, route mat.

**Preparation:**  
Place station signs away from the routes.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children decide where stations should go and explain by moving people or vehicles. They can create a start and end point for each route.

**Teacher Notes:**  
K2 explanation can be short: “train here,” “people wait,” “car road.”
