# Germany · K3 · Lesson 4
## Germany Mini Museum — Build, Label and Present

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K3
- Lesson: 4 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children create a mini museum that organises the Germany unit into clear sections: transport systems, design and engineering, forests and stories, architecture or clocks. The focus is on curating, labelling, explaining, and presenting what the class has made.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- organise Germany-related work into categories for a class mini museum;
- create simple labels, arrows, or captions to support audience understanding;
- present one selected object, model, picture, or story element using short explanatory language.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Which section does this belong to?
- Add a label so visitors can understand.
- What did you make?
- Why did you put it here?
- Present one thing from the museum.

Possible Child Response:
- transport / forest / bridge / clock / castle / story
- this is my bridge / it is strong / it goes here
- because / first / next / I made

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Museum Category Set-Up

**Purpose:**  
To help children classify and arrange accumulated work.

**Materials:**  
Children’s models, drawings, photos, story maps, transport routes, category signs, display tables or floor mats.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare four possible museum sections: Transport, Design, Forest Stories, Buildings and Clocks.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose one piece of work and decide where it belongs. They place it in a section and check whether the section has similar items.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Some pieces can fit more than one category. Use this as an opportunity for discussion rather than correction.

### Activity 2: Label and Arrow Workshop

**Purpose:**  
To support early curation and explanatory display.

**Materials:**  
Small cards, markers, printed words or picture labels, arrows, tape, child photos.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare label templates with space for a word, drawing, or teacher-scribed sentence.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children make labels for selected objects. They can write, trace, draw, or dictate. Add arrows showing movement or function, such as route direction or bridge support.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Labels should be functional, not decorative only. They help visitors understand what they are seeing.

### Activity 3: Mini Museum Tour

**Purpose:**  
To give K3 children a meaningful presentation experience.

**Materials:**  
Completed mini museum, pointer, visitor badges or simple route cards.

**Preparation:**  
Arrange the display so small groups can move safely through it.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children take turns presenting one section or one object. They name the object, explain one feature, and show how it works or why it belongs in that section.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Presentation can be short. Prioritise clarity, confidence, and connection to the child’s own work.
