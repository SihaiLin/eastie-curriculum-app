# Germany · K3 Overview

## 1. Country Character

Germany is a useful K3 theme because it supports project-based thinking about design, systems, engineering, architecture, forests, stories, and music. Children can organise information about how people move, build, make, tell stories, and design useful objects. The unit should avoid becoming a fact-heavy geography or history lesson. It should focus on Germany as a place of routes, structures, invention, craftsmanship, forests, and cultural stories.

## 2. Grade-Level Lens

For K3 children, Germany should be explored through organising, explaining, designing, and presenting. Children can create a route map, compare transport systems, design a bridge or town, connect forests with traditional stories, and curate a small “Made and Designed in Germany” display. The main lens is: “How can people design systems, places, and objects?”

## 3. Classroom Setup and Key Elements

The classroom can include a Germany project area with maps at a simple level, transport images, car and train model materials, bridge and architecture references, Black Forest imagery, Grimm-style story props, clock and gear visuals, and display boards for children’s designs. K3 children can use labels, arrows, categories, and short presentations.

## 4. Four-Lesson Map

### Lesson 1: Germany on the Move — Routes and Systems
Children organise cars, trains, roads, railways, bridges, tunnels, and stations into a simple transport system and explain how people move from place to place.

### Lesson 2: Design and Engineering — Bridges, Cars and Useful Objects
Children investigate design problems such as making a bridge strong, making a car move, or choosing materials for a route, then test and explain their designs.

### Lesson 3: Forests, Stories and Cultural Imagination
Children explore German forest settings and child-friendly traditional story motifs through maps, story paths, character cards, and theatre-style retelling.

### Lesson 4: Germany Mini Museum — Build, Label and Present
Children create a small project display with transport, forest, architecture, clock, and design elements, then organise and present selected pieces to classmates.
