# Germany · K3 · Lesson 2
## Design and Engineering — Bridges, Cars and Useful Objects

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K3
- Lesson: 2 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore Germany through design and engineering thinking. They investigate simple design problems: how to make a bridge stronger, how to make a vehicle move farther, and how to choose materials for useful objects. The lesson emphasises testing, improving, and explaining.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- investigate a simple design problem using building materials, vehicles, bridges, or moving parts;
- test and improve a structure or object based on what happens during play;
- explain one design choice using simple evidence from testing.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- What problem are we solving?
- Test it. Does it work?
- How can we make it stronger?
- What did you change?
- Explain your design choice.

Possible Child Response:
- strong / weak / high / low / stable / move
- it works / it falls / we changed this
- because / first / then / next

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Bridge Strength Challenge

**Purpose:**  
To introduce engineering as testing and improving.

**Materials:**  
Wooden blocks, cardboard strips, paper tubes, small toy cars, counters or light weights.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a gap between two low block supports.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children build a bridge across the gap, test whether a toy car can cross, then improve the bridge if it bends or falls. Encourage them to compare wide/narrow, high/low, many/few supports.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Focus on process, not winning. Failed designs are useful because they give children something to improve.

### Activity 2: Car Roll Test

**Purpose:**  
To connect vehicle movement with surface, slope, and design.

**Materials:**  
Toy cars, ramps, cardboard roads, different surfaces such as felt, paper, mat, or smooth board.

**Preparation:**  
Set up two or three short test lanes.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children roll cars down lanes and observe which car moves farther or stops sooner. They can change the slope or surface and test again.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Use simple language: farther, slower, smoother, rougher. Avoid turning it into a formal physics lesson.

### Activity 3: Useful Object Design Board

**Purpose:**  
To help children organise design thinking visually.

**Materials:**  
Paper board, drawing tools, photo cards of bridges, cars, clocks, tools, arrows, labels.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a three-part board: problem, design, test.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose one design problem from the lesson and draw or place photos to show what they tried. They add arrows or labels to explain their process.

**Teacher Notes:**  
K3 children can begin representing thinking, but drawings and teacher-scribed words are acceptable.
