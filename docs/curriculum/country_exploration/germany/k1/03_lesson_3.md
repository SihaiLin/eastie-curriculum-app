# Germany · K1 · Lesson 3
## Forests, Houses and Story Paths

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 3 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore Germany through forests, small houses, paths, and gentle story settings. The lesson uses trees, animal figures, houses, and path-making to create a calm forest-town experience. It should feel like entering a story place, not studying folklore in detail.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice trees, paths, houses, and simple forest animals in a Germany story setting;
- move along a pretend forest path and place objects in a shared scene;
- create or decorate a small house or tree element for the class display.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- This is a forest.
- I see trees. I see a house.
- Walk on the path.
- Put the house near the trees.
- Who lives here?

Possible Child Response:
- tree / house / path / animal
- near / here
- walking, placing, choosing, pointing

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Forest Path Walk

**Purpose:**  
To let children experience the forest theme through movement and spatial awareness.

**Materials:**  
Green fabric, paper trees, floor path markers, animal figures, soft music if available.

**Preparation:**  
Create a short winding path through paper or block trees.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children walk slowly along the path. They stop to find a tree, animal, or house picture. The teacher narrates simple actions: walk, stop, look, touch gently.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep the atmosphere calm. Avoid scary forest storytelling.

### Activity 2: Build a Small Forest House

**Purpose:**  
To connect forest images with concrete building.

**Materials:**  
Wooden blocks, cardboard house shapes, roof triangles, paper doors and windows.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a few simple house bases and roof pieces.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children build or decorate small houses. They can add a roof, door, window, and place the house near trees on the forest mat.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Focus on visible parts: roof, door, window. Do not require symmetrical or realistic houses.

### Activity 3: Story Object Basket

**Purpose:**  
To support symbolic play with forest and house props.

**Materials:**  
Basket with small trees, animals, people figures, house cards, path cards, fabric pieces.

**Preparation:**  
Place the basket beside the forest mat.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose one object from the basket and place it in the scene. The teacher briefly describes each placement: “A tree here,” “A house near the path,” “An animal in the forest.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
This is not a story-retelling task. It is early scene-building and shared pretend play.
