# Germany · K1 · Lesson 4
## Build a Mini Germany Town

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 4 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children bring together the K1 Germany experience by building a mini town with roads, tracks, cars, trains, houses, trees, and a castle. The focus is on shared construction, placement, and simple walk-through sharing.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- combine transport, forest, house, and castle elements into one shared Germany town;
- participate in placing, building, moving, or choosing materials for the town;
- share their work through pointing, naming, showing, or simple movement.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Let’s build a Germany town.
- Put the road here.
- The train goes to the station.
- The house is near the trees.
- Show me what you made.

Possible Child Response:
- car / train / house / tree / castle
- here / mine / road
- pointing, showing, moving vehicles, placing blocks

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Town Planning Mat

**Purpose:**  
To help children organise previous lesson materials into one shared space.

**Materials:**  
Large floor mat or paper base, tape roads, train tracks, blocks, tree props, house pieces, toy vehicles.

**Preparation:**  
Divide the mat lightly into road area, train area, forest area, and town area using tape or fabric.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Invite children to choose one material and place it on the mat. The teacher supports with simple prompts: “road here,” “trees here,” “train here.” Children can then move cars and trains through the town.

**Teacher Notes:**  
The mat does not need to be realistic. The goal is visible child participation and shared placement.

### Activity 2: Castle and House Build

**Purpose:**  
To add landmark-like structures in an age-appropriate way.

**Materials:**  
Wooden blocks, Lego, cardboard towers, paper roofs, simple castle image.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare a small image of a castle and a tray of building materials.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children work with the teacher to build one castle and several small houses. They add them to the town beside the road, track, or forest.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Avoid over-directing the final form. Towers, walls, and roofs are enough.

### Activity 3: Mini Germany Walk-Through

**Purpose:**  
To let children revisit and share their completed class display.

**Materials:**  
Completed town, pointer stick or small flag, camera for documentation.

**Preparation:**  
Clear space around the display so children can walk safely.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children walk around the town with the teacher. Each child can point to one thing they made or like. The teacher names it and connects it to the theme: car, train, house, tree, castle.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Do not require children to present formally. Showing and pointing are appropriate K1 sharing.
