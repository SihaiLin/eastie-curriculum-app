# Germany · K1 · Lesson 1
## Cars, Roads and Moving Around

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 1 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children meet Germany through cars, roads, wheels, and movement. The lesson focuses on direct play with vehicle toys and visible road lines. Children notice fast/slow, stop/go, straight/curved, and near/far through action rather than explanation.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice cars, wheels, roads, tunnels, and simple traffic movement as part of the Germany theme;
- respond to movement prompts through pushing, stopping, choosing, pointing, or single words;
- help create a small road play area using vehicles, tape roads, blocks, and signs.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- Look, this is Germany.
- I see a car. I see a road.
- Go slowly. Stop here.
- The road is straight / curved.
- Put the car on the road.

Possible Child Response:
- car / road / wheel / stop / go
- fast / slow
- pointing, pushing, stopping, placing vehicles

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Road Tape Discovery

**Purpose:**  
To let children enter the theme through clear floor movement and vehicle play.

**Materials:**  
Masking tape or road tape, toy cars, small stop signs, cardboard tunnels, blocks.

**Preparation:**  
Create a simple road line on the floor with one straight road, one curved road, one tunnel, and one stopping point.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Invite children to place cars on the road. Model slow driving, stopping, turning, and going through the tunnel. Let children choose cars and move them along the path one at a time or in pairs.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep traffic rules very simple. The goal is controlled movement and noticing, not formal road safety teaching.

### Activity 2: Wheel Hunt

**Purpose:**  
To focus children’s attention on wheels as a concrete feature.

**Materials:**  
Toy cars, train pieces, wheeled classroom objects, picture cards showing wheels.

**Preparation:**  
Place wheeled objects on a mat and a few non-wheeled objects nearby for contrast.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Ask children to find wheels. They can touch, point, roll, or choose objects with wheels. Then let them test which objects can roll along a short road.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Accept non-verbal responses. Use repeated language: “wheel,” “roll,” “stop,” “go.”

### Activity 3: Car Garage Corner

**Purpose:**  
To begin building a shared Germany transport corner.

**Materials:**  
Toy cars, small boxes as garages, road mat or tape road, blocks, labels or picture signs.

**Preparation:**  
Prepare several box garages beside the road area.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children choose a car, drive it on the road, and park it in a garage. They can add blocks as buildings or tunnels. Photograph the completed garage corner for the class display.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Use turn-taking if many children want the same vehicle. Avoid competitive racing.
