# Germany · K1 · Lesson 2
## Trains, Tracks and Stations

## 1. Lesson Identity

- Country / Place: Germany
- Grade: K1
- Lesson: 2 of 4

## 2. Lesson Focus

Children explore Germany through trains, tracks, and stations. The focus is on joining pieces, following a route, moving trains safely, and noticing long/short, together/apart, and in/out through play.

## 3. Learning Objectives

Children will:
- notice trains, tracks, stations, and tunnels as concrete transport elements;
- participate in building or using a simple train route with adult support;
- respond to route language through placing, joining, moving, waiting, or single words.

## 4. Teacher Language

Teacher Input:
- This is a train.
- Put the train on the track.
- Join the tracks together.
- The train goes to the station.
- Wait. Now go.

Possible Child Response:
- train / track / station / tunnel
- go / stop / long
- joining, placing, moving, waiting

## 5. Suggested Games and Activities

### Activity 1: Track Builders

**Purpose:**  
To support hand-eye coordination and shared construction through train-track play.

**Materials:**  
Wooden or plastic train tracks, toy trains, small station blocks, tunnel pieces.

**Preparation:**  
Place track pieces in baskets and prepare a simple example track for reference.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Invite children to help join track pieces. The teacher supports by holding pieces steady and naming actions: join, turn, long, short. Once a route is ready, children move trains around the track.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Do not require all children to build a complete route. Partial joining and placing are valid participation.

### Activity 2: Station Stop Game

**Purpose:**  
To practise controlled movement and waiting.

**Materials:**  
Toy train, station cards or blocks, small figures, stop/go cards.

**Preparation:**  
Set up two or three stations along the track.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Children move the train from station to station. At each station, they stop, place a small figure, or wave. The teacher uses simple prompts: “Stop at the station,” “People get in,” “Train goes.”

**Teacher Notes:**  
Keep the rhythm predictable. This helps K1 children join without needing much verbal output.

### Activity 3: Tunnel and Bridge Train Play

**Purpose:**  
To let children notice over, under, in, and out through train movement.

**Materials:**  
Blocks, cardboard tunnels, low bridge pieces, train tracks, toy trains.

**Preparation:**  
Build one tunnel and one low bridge along the track.

**How to Play / Do:**  
Invite children to send the train through the tunnel and under or over the bridge depending on the setup. Children can add more blocks to make the tunnel longer.

**Teacher Notes:**  
Use prepositions as exposure only. Children can respond by moving the train correctly.
